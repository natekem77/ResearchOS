# Retinal Organoid Patterning Review

Title: Retinal organoid patterning signals in early differentiation
Authors: ResearchOS Demo Literature Team
Year: 2024
Journal: Demo Journal of Retinal Models
DOI: 10.0000/researchos.demo.retina.2024

Abstract:
Retinal organoid differentiation depends on coordinated signaling and careful
timing of early patterning cues. BMP4 exposure during early neural induction has
been reported to influence optic vesicle-like morphology, while SHH pathway
modulation with SAG is often used to test ventral patterning effects. Markers
such as SIX6, BRN3B, PAX6, VSX2, RAX, and CRX are commonly used to evaluate
retinal progenitor identity and retinal ganglion cell differentiation.

Keywords: retinal organoid, BMP4, SAG, SIX6, BRN3B, PAX6, VSX2, immunostaining,
brightfield, RNA-seq

Notes:
- BMP4 timing should be interpreted in relation to aggregate size and starting
  colony quality.
- SAG experiments should include vehicle controls such as DMSO.
- BRN3B staining is sensitive to background and should include secondary-only
  controls.
