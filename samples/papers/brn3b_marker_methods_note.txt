Title: BRN3B immunostaining considerations in retinal organoids
Authors: ResearchOS Demo Literature Team
Year: 2023
Journal: Demo Methods Notes
DOI: 10.0000/researchos.demo.brn3b.2023

Abstract:
BRN3B is used as a retinal ganglion cell marker in retinal organoid cultures.
Interpretation benefits from co-staining with progenitor or retinal identity
markers such as SIX6 and PAX6. DAPI is commonly used for nuclei. Confocal
imaging and careful blocking controls help reduce false positive interpretation
when background signal is high.

Keywords: BRN3B, SIX6, PAX6, DAPI, retinal ganglion cell, immunostaining,
confocal
