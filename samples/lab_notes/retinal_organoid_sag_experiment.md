# Retinal Organoid SAG Experiment

Date: 2026-06-12

Objective: Test whether SAG exposure improves ventral retinal organoid patterning
without reducing overall organoid viability.

Experimental setup:

- Cell line: SIX6 reporter iPSC line.
- Differentiation day: D18 to D24 exposure window.
- Treatment: 100 nM SAG added daily during media changes.
- Control: matched DMSO vehicle.
- Readouts: brightfield morphology, SIX6 fluorescence, and BRN3B staining at D32.

Observations:

The SAG-treated organoids showed slightly smoother neuroepithelial rims by D24.
Vehicle controls had more irregular edges and occasional cystic regions. No
obvious increase in cell death was observed during daily checks.

Preliminary interpretation:

SAG may improve early patterning consistency in this batch. The effect should be
confirmed with a larger replicate count and quantified SIX6 intensity analysis.
