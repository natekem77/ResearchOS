# BMP4 Protocol Note

Date: 2026-06-05
Researcher: Protocol Team
Organoid batch: RO-BMP4-setup

Purpose: Record the BMP4 pulse protocol used for early retinal differentiation.

Protocol:

1. Start with healthy iPSC aggregates at differentiation day 6.
2. Prepare fresh neural induction medium with BMP4 at 1.5 nM.
3. Apply BMP4 pulse from D6 through D9.
4. Replace medium every 24 hours during the pulse.
5. Remove BMP4 at D9 and continue standard retinal differentiation medium.

Important handling notes:

- Avoid overgrown starting colonies because they produce uneven aggregate sizes.
- Warm media fully before feeding to reduce aggregate stress.
- Record aggregate size distribution before BMP4 exposure.

Expected result:

The BMP4 pulse should improve optic vesicle-like morphology by D16 to D20 when
starting aggregates are uniform.
