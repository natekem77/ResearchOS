# SIX6/BRN3B Staining Result

Date: 2026-06-28

Sample: Retinal organoids fixed at D32 after SAG treatment comparison.

Markers:

- SIX6 for retinal progenitor identity.
- BRN3B for retinal ganglion cell differentiation.
- DAPI for nuclei.

Result summary:

SIX6 signal was broadly present across the outer neuroepithelial regions in both
vehicle and SAG-treated organoids. BRN3B-positive cells were sparse but more
organized near the inner surface of the SAG-treated group.

Notes:

The staining background was higher than expected in the BRN3B channel. The next
run should include a longer blocking step and a secondary-only control.

Interpretation:

The result is consistent with retinal lineage progression, but BRN3B conclusions
should remain cautious until staining background is reduced.
